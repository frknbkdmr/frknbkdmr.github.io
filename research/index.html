Research

Three open questions and two abstracts already published. The open questions share a method: all of them are about how a brain weighs what it already believes against what it is currently being told.

Open

Residency thesis · BAP04-B-2026-6538 · in preparation

Belief updating and sensory attenuation in schizophrenia

Whether delusions track a failure of belief revision, a failure to attenuate self-generated sensation, or both. A beads task and a force-matching task in the same people.

Theory · in design

Akathisia as active inference

Akathisia drives movement that rarely settles it. Active inference offers an account of why.

With the Department of Neurology · in collection

Effort and reward in multiple sclerosis

Fatigue and depression overlap on every questionnaire that tries to separate them. Effort-based decision making offers a behavioural separation.
